## The failure

When an operator builds and maintains multiple complex AI systems at once — quantitative strategy and verification engines, multi-disciplinary research and advocacy teams, pattern and cycle analysis suites, and full-stack operational terminals — engineering each project's prompts, agents, and skills ad hoc inside its own repository repeats `P-001` across the portfolio. Every new thread re-invents agent roles, drops state between sessions, accepts unverified summary numbers without out-of-sample splits, or risks crossing private operational facts into public trees.

## The mechanism

**Anime Stack Project Turbo** elevates the upstream eight-seat house (`README.md`, `skills/house/SKILL.md`) from a single-repository coding room into a central **AI Project Engineering System**:

1. **Mei Sakuraba** audits model regime shifts, host tool surfaces, and context-window budgets (`principles/doctrine/guard-the-context-window.md`) — enforcing a `< 15 KB` operational ceiling (`< 24 KB` host byte cap) across live rulebooks (`AGENTS.md`), `.agents/` subagent/skill prompts, and session handoffs plus a `<= 140`-line ceiling on active collaboration boards via `*_ARCHIVE_*.md` rotation, quarantining cloud-sync conflict copies, and requiring bounded top-slice (`Lines 1-140`) reads on large ledgers and `100 KB+` manuscripts.
2. **Elo Star** enforces the Dual-Gate Measurement Bar (`docs/measurement.md`): (a) **Causal Live-Parity Evaluation Hygiene (`Laws 1-7`)** — requiring Ask-Entry/Bid-Exit spread modeling, intra-interval high/low excursion checks before targets, causal rolling anchors without full-session look-ahead, adverse-selection-accurate passive limit fills on losing paths, multi-month sample/quality floors (`N >= 100+` / `>= 60`, `PF >= 1.50` / `> 1.29`, `<= 3-4` gates), chronological stability splits, and explicit physical or clinical bottleneck denominators; and (b) **Code & Runtime Execution Invariants (`Laws E1-E7`)** — auditing repo-wide call-graph & bytecode symbol parity, fail-closed `None` standby on unpolled/blank state, `math.floor` fee-capped unit sizing, UI display-buffer vs. persisted-config separation, canonical timezone & re-entrant lock safety, single-instance kernel mutexes & single-source orderbooks, and `< 15 KB` / `<= 140`-line context budgets.
