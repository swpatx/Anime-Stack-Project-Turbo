## The failure

When an operator runs multiple specialized AI workspaces — software builder projects, independent forensic watchdog projects, multi-disciplinary clinical/legal research houses, resilience engineering planners, and executive operations systems — authoring prompts and skills ad hoc repeats `P-001`, `P-002`, and `A-005` across the portfolio. Prompts bloat past host ceilings, foreign agent files pollute unrelated workspaces, sessions start cold after compaction, builder agents grade their own gates or invent unapproved criteria, or meta-engineering hubs bleed into debugging downstream domain code.

## The mechanism

**Anime Stack Project Turbo** elevates the upstream eight-seat house (`README.md`, `skills/house/SKILL.md`) into a dedicated **Meta-Agent & Customized Per-Project `AnimeStack` Engineering System** whose boundary stops strictly at agent, skill, setting, anti-bloat, and context-recovery engineering:

1. **Mei Sakuraba** stewards host climate and context budgets (`principles/doctrine/guard-the-context-window.md`) — enforcing a `< 15 KB` hard ceiling (`< 12.5 KB` operational target inside `< 24 KB` host limits) across all `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `.agents/` prompts, and handoffs, capping active collaboration boards/handoffs at `<= 140` lines via `*_ARCHIVE_*.md` rotation, quarantining conflict copies, enforcing bounded `Lines 1-140` reads on `100 KB+` files, and keeping `.agents/` rosters pure per workspace.
2. **Elo Star** measures engineered agent houses (`docs/measurement.md`) — auditing exact file byte/line budgets, subagent dispatch counts (`0` solo bypasses), cold-thread recovery completeness, primary-source/realized-data honesty rules inside customized target prompts, Operator-owned criteria locks, and the 40-row repository measurement table.
3. **MechaPip** retrieves architectural prior art (`patterns/registry.json`) before any agent, skill, or customization ships — refusing `P-001` inline router escape hatches, `P-002` overlapping persona/foreign-roster bloat, `P-003` wall-clock-drifting generated surfaces, `P-004` self-counted gate literals, and `A-002`/`A-005` scope bleed between meta-agent engineering and downstream project auditing.
4. **Yui Aizawa** architects deterministic prompt mechanisms and pre-registered falsifiers (`docs/claim-standard.md`) — customized 8-seat `AnimeStack` editions (`One box, one mechanism`), mandatory subagent router locks, structured `<= 140`-line session-pickup handoffs with versioned active-vs-pending state locks, Builder-vs-Watchdog `Zero Self-Grading` separation, and `Ask First` Operator-consent rules.
5. **Niko Hoshino** cages physical workspace topology — isolating each project's customized `.agents/` directory (`0` foreign copies), neutralizing discovery filenames (`retired_dot_agents`, `*_RETIRED.md`) inside `retired*` archive folders so retired prompts never ghost-autoload, preventing active-thread collisions, and barring the meta-hub from touching downstream application code.
6. **Rin Takamiya** enforces pre-flight vetoes (`END`) against `< 15 KB` / `<= 140`-line budget breaches, retired-folder ghost autoloading, unbounded `100 KB+` reads, `P-001` inline bypasses, scope bleed into downstream project debugging, unapproved criteria invention, contract/voice/site drift, and any hit on the two-tier zero-leak gate (`scripts/check-leak`).
7. **Aria Kanzaki** executes deterministic pack generation and release (`scripts/gen-agent-dialects`, `scripts/gen-dsh-house`, `scripts/gen-site` with release-date-anchored record rendering) gated by the full static, contract, measurement, scorecard, installer, test, and zero-leak (`--tree` and `--history`) suite.
8. **Reika Kurogane** frames the one-sentence mandate for every customized `AnimeStack` system, upholds the meta-engineering scope boundary and `One box, one mechanism`, and signs the final verdict (`SHIP`, `RETIRE`, or `STAND DOWN`).

## Why it parks and does not ship

Under the house's own Claim Standard (`docs/claim-standard.md`), an architectural claim about outcome quality across downstream engineering tasks cannot ship on the day it is carved. The static gates (`scripts/check-leak`, `scripts/check-contract`, `scripts/check-measurement`, `scripts/check-claim`) verify the tree's body today; the outcome difference across ten fresh multi-project AI engineering tasks is parked with a dated review (`2026-12-07`).
