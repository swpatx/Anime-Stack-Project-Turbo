## The failure

When an operator builds and maintains multiple complex AI systems at once — quantitative strategy and verification engines, multi-disciplinary research and advocacy teams, pattern and cycle analysis suites, and full-stack operational terminals — engineering each project's prompts, agents, and skills ad hoc inside its own repository repeats `P-001` across the portfolio. Every new thread re-invents agent roles, drops state between sessions, accepts unverified summary numbers without out-of-sample splits, or risks crossing private operational facts into public trees.

## The mechanism

**Anime Stack Project Turbo** elevates the upstream eight-seat house (`README.md`, `skills/house/SKILL.md`) from a single-repository coding room into a central **AI Project Engineering System**:

1. **Mei Sakuraba** audits model regime shifts, host tool surfaces, and context-window budgets (`principles/doctrine/guard-the-context-window.md`) so downstream agents read deep records on demand rather than bloating base context.
2. **Elo Star** enforces the measurement bar (`docs/measurement.md`) across downstream evaluation skills — requiring chronological first-half vs. second-half stability splits, exact cost/friction accounting, and single-table scorecards.
3. **MechaPip** runs cross-project pattern retrieval (`patterns/registry.json`) before any new subagent or skill is created, refusing lookalike persona duplication when an existing specialist or mechanism already owns the lane.
4. **Yui Aizawa** designs deterministic prompt mechanisms, state locks, and pre-registered falsifiers (`docs/claim-standard.md`) before any downstream module or skill is trusted.
5. **Niko Hoshino** cages the physical architecture — workspace rulebooks, subagent routing tables, startup/end-of-turn session handoffs, and strict file/settings isolation.
6. **Rin Takamiya** enforces destructive-command vetoes, cold verification passes on diffs without build reasoning, and the two-tier zero-leak gate (`scripts/check-leak`).
7. **Aria Kanzaki** packages turn-key release pipelines — lockstep version stamps, headless test gates, executable builds, and atomic releases.
8. **Reika Kurogane** frames the one-sentence mandate for every engineered system and signs the final verdict (`SHIP`, `RETIRE`, or `STAND DOWN`).

## Why it parks and does not ship

Under the house's own Claim Standard (`docs/claim-standard.md`), an architectural claim about outcome quality across downstream engineering tasks cannot ship on the day it is carved. The static gates (`scripts/check-leak`, `scripts/check-contract`, `scripts/check-measurement`, `scripts/check-claim`) verify the tree's body today; the outcome difference across ten fresh multi-project AI engineering tasks is parked with a dated review (`2026-12-07`).
